How Dupe Sweep works

On this page: Install · A safe first run · What counts as a match · Which record is kept · Undo and the merge log · Plans and keys · Switching from another tool · How we tested · Leaving

Install

  1. Press Connect HubSpot and choose your account. You need to be a Super Admin or have App Marketplace permission.
  2. HubSpot shows a permission screen. Dupe Sweep asks to view and edit contacts and companies, and nothing else: no deals, tickets, emails or users.
  3. Approve, and you land in the app. Open Contacts or Companies and press Scan.

About the warning. Dupe Sweep is new and is not yet listed on the HubSpot App Marketplace, so HubSpot may show a notice on the install screen that the app has not been reviewed by HubSpot. That is true, and it is fair to pause on it. Before you approve you can check three things: the permission list is only contacts and companies; the privacy policy says what we store (no copy of your records); and you can revoke access at any time under Settings, Integrations, Connected apps in HubSpot, or press Disconnect and delete in the app to remove everything we hold. If you would rather wait for the Marketplace listing, that is a reasonable choice.

A safe first run

Scanning changes nothing in HubSpot, so you can run it and just read the results. If you decide to merge, start small: open Compare on a few sets, press Not a duplicate on any you disagree with, untick the rest, and merge three or four sets. Look at the records in HubSpot, then do the rest. The free plan includes 25 merges a month, which is enough for this.

Scanning

Dupe Sweep reads your records through HubSpot in pages of 100 and compares them in your browser, so keep the tab open while it runs. In our tests 4,659 contacts loaded in 14 seconds; a 50,000 contact account should take a few minutes, longer if HubSpot asks the app to slow down (it waits and carries on). Results show 200 sets at a time with a Show more button.

What counts as a match

For contacts: same email, same phone, or a similar name with something to back it up (same company, same work email domain, same phone). Nicknames, small typos, swapped first and last names and initials are handled. The same name on its own is never enough to merge. For companies: same website domain, or a similar name together with the same phone, city or domain. Each set in the app spells out its reasons in a sentence, and hovering a tag explains it.

90 and upReady to merge. Ticked by default; untick any set.
70 to 89Review. A link is missing or different. Nothing is ticked. Merge a pair, or mark it Not a duplicate.
Below 70Not shown. The summary tells you how many were hidden.

Which record is kept

The record with the most filled-in fields. If they tie, the older one. Each set says which record is kept and the Compare view explains why. In Compare, amber cells differ from the kept record and blue cells hold a value only the merged-in record has. HubSpot gives the combined record the activity and associations of both and keeps the kept record's property values. Anything that exists only on the merged-in record is still saved in the Merge log. HubSpot also gives the kept record a new ID after each merge; the old IDs open the new record.

Undo and the merge log

HubSpot does not allow a merge to be reversed, by any app or by HubSpot itself. Dupe Sweep cannot change that, so it does three things instead: nothing merges until you confirm a list that names each record, the free plan lets you test on a handful, and every merge goes into the log with the names, both IDs and every value the merged-in record had.

To get a record back: open the CSV from the Merge log tab, find the row (search the name or the old ID), copy the values from the merged_record_data column, and create a new contact or company in HubSpot. It gets a new ID and starts without its old activity history, which stays on the kept record. Some tools, like Dedupely, offer a 30-day undo; Dupe Sweep does not.

Plans and keys

Free is 25 merges a month, resetting on the 1st (UTC), with unlimited scanning and review. The app tells you before you start a merge if your selection is larger than what is left, and the confirm button then reads "Merge the first 25". Pro is $9 a month or $90 a year for one HubSpot account. After paying on Polar you get a license key by email (check spam). Paste it in Plan and account and press Apply key; Pro turns on at once. One key works on two HubSpot accounts, so you can move between a sandbox and production. Cancel from the link in your Polar receipt; the account returns to Free. If a key will not apply, email us.

Switching from Dedupely, Insycle or HubSpot's tool

There is nothing to import: Dupe Sweep reads your records from HubSpot, so anything you already merged is simply gone from the list. Rules, schedules and automations in the other tool do not carry over, and Dupe Sweep has none of its own, so keep the other tool for those. Do not run two tools merging the same records at the same time. If you used HubSpot's native Not a duplicate list, it is not read by Dupe Sweep; mark those pairs again here.

How we tested

The matcher was run on generated contacts and companies with known answers: typos, nicknames, five phone formats, Gmail aliases, company suffixes, and look-alike people who must not merge (same name at different companies, relatives sharing a phone, Ryan and Bryan, franchises). Live runs seeded a HubSpot developer test account with about 4,600 and 3,300 contacts and 2,000 and 1,000 companies, scanned and merged through the real API: no wrong merges in the 90-and-up tier, 85 to 86 percent of contact duplicates and 94 to 95 percent of company duplicates found in that tier, 98 percent or more with review. On the same data HubSpot's own list held 2,243 and 1,297 contact pairs where the data had 672 and 467 real duplicate pairs, about two thirds of them wrong; on companies it was accurate but found about 78 percent. 600 offline runs gave one wrong pair in 115,886. Limits: the data is generated, a real account is messier, and no outside user has tested it yet. We will update this page as real accounts report back.

Leaving

Press Disconnect and delete in Plan and account to remove the connection, the merge log and the not-a-duplicate list from our server. Then uninstall the app in HubSpot under Settings, Integrations, Connected apps. Your HubSpot records are never touched by this. Download the CSV first if you want to keep the log.
