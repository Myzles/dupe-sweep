How Dupe Sweep works

Set up

  1. Press Connect HubSpot and pick your account. You need to be a Super Admin or have App Marketplace access.
  2. Approve the access. Dupe Sweep asks to read and edit contacts and companies, and nothing else.
  3. You land in the app. Open the Contacts or Companies tab and press Scan.

Scanning

Dupe Sweep reads your records through HubSpot in pages of 100 and compares them in your browser. A 10,000 record account takes a few minutes. Close the tab and nothing is left running.

What counts as a match

For contacts: same email, same phone, or a similar name with something to back it up (same company, same work email domain, same phone). Nicknames, small typos, swapped first and last names and initials are all handled. The same name on its own is never enough to merge. For companies: same website domain, or a similar name together with the same phone, city or domain.

Confidence

90 and upReady to merge. Selected by default.
70 to 89Review. You choose to merge or mark it as not a duplicate.
Below 70Not shown.

Which record is kept

The record with the most filled-in fields. If they tie, the older one. HubSpot then combines the activity and keeps the kept record's property values.

Merging

Select the sets and press Merge selected. Each merge is a call to HubSpot's merge API. It cannot be undone in HubSpot. The Merge log tab lists every merge and has a CSV download with the values of every merged-away record.

Plans

Free is 25 merges a month. Pro removes the limit. Paste your key in Plan and account. One key works on one HubSpot account (two activations are allowed so you can move between a sandbox and production).

Leaving

Use Disconnect and delete in Plan and account to remove everything we store. Then uninstall the app in HubSpot under Settings, Integrations, Connected apps.
