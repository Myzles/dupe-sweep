Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026. Dupe Sweep is run by Swag Studios (contact MylesCar2828@outlook.com).

What we store

  • Your HubSpot account ID, domain and the email of the user who installed the app.
  • The HubSpot access and refresh tokens, encrypted.
  • The merge log: record IDs, the time, the confidence, and the property values of the record that was merged away (names, emails, phones, company details).
  • Pairs you marked as not a duplicate (record IDs).
  • Your license key and how many merges you made this month.

What we do not store

Your contact and company records are read through our server while you scan and are matched in your browser. We do not keep them. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising or to train models.

Who handles data

  • Cloudflare, which hosts the app and its database (United States).
  • Polar, which processes payments and issues license keys.
  • HubSpot, which is where your data lives.

How long we keep it

Until you press Disconnect and delete, or uninstall the app (we delete the data when HubSpot rejects our token). You can also email us to delete everything. Payment records stay with Polar as the law requires.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Tokens are encrypted at rest. Sessions expire after 12 hours. The app only asks for the permissions it needs: read and write contacts and companies.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data or for it to be deleted by emailing us. If you are in the EU or UK, we act as a processor for the data in your HubSpot account.
