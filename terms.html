The service

Dupe Sweep finds likely duplicate records in your HubSpot account and, when you tell it to, merges them using HubSpot's merge API.

You are in control

Merges happen only when you press a merge button. HubSpot merges cannot be undone. You are responsible for checking matches before you merge. We do not promise that every duplicate will be found or that every match is correct.
