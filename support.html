Common questions

Can I undo a merge? HubSpot does not offer this for any app. The merge log has the values of the merged-away record so you can recreate it by hand.

A set I expected is not in the list. Matches below 70 are not shown. If two records share nothing but a name, Dupe Sweep leaves them alone on purpose.

The scan says HubSpot asked us to slow down. That is normal on large accounts. The scan waits and carries on.

My license key says it is already in use. A key can be active on two HubSpot accounts. Email us and we will reset it.

Refunds. Email within 14 days of a payment and we will refund it.
