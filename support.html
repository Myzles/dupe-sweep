Support

Email swagstudios.helpdesk@outlook.com. A person replies within two business days. Please include your HubSpot account ID (shown at the top of the app) and what you expected to happen. Dupe Sweep is run by Swag Studios, a small independent studio.

Common questions

Can I undo a merge? HubSpot does not offer this for any app. The merge log has every value of the merged-away record so you can recreate it by hand: how.

HubSpot shows a warning when I install. The app is not on the Marketplace yet, so HubSpot may say it has not been reviewed. See what to check before you approve.

A set I expected is not in the list. Matches below 70 are not shown, and two records that share nothing but a name are left alone on purpose. Send us the two record IDs and we will look at why.

The kept record has a new ID after a merge. HubSpot gives the merged record a new ID. The old IDs still open the new record. If another tool stores HubSpot record IDs, check that it follows the change.

The scan says HubSpot asked us to slow down. That is normal on large accounts. The scan waits and carries on. Keep the tab open.

The app says I am signed out. Sessions last 12 hours. Press Connect HubSpot again; your merge log and plan are kept.

My free merges ran out. They come back on the 1st (UTC). Pro removes the limit.

My license key will not apply. Check you pasted the whole key (it starts with DSWP-). A key works on two HubSpot accounts; if it is already used on two, email us and we will reset it.

Refunds. Reply to your Polar receipt within 7 days of a payment and we will refund it in full.

Delete my data. Press Disconnect and delete in the app, or email us.
